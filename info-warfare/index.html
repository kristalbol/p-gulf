Western outlets (CNN, NYT, BBC, Reuters) maintain higher credibility but face increasing audience skepticism, particularly from younger demographics and non-Western consumers. Western Alliance Silo: US/UK/EU outlets emphasizing precision, minimal civilian casualties, and Iranian aggression narrative. Credibility declining due to shifting narratives and visible gaps between rhetoric and observable reality (satellite imagery, independent casualty estimates).

Anti-US Coalition Silo: Russia, China, Iran, and aligned media amplifying themes of American imperialism and destabilization. Credibility low in Western markets but increasing traction in Global South and among anti-intervention constituencies in the West.

The result: truth is increasingly determined not by evidence but by which information silo a consumer inhabits.
